# Clarity & Decision — AI EHub

Four mentoring tools for thinking a problem through, whatever the subject. Use the other AI EHub areas when the question is about a specific subject (finance, marketing, starting a business); use these when the question cuts across subjects or the goal isn't clear yet.

Each tool is a skill: ask for it by name in any chat (for example, "Use Decision Coach to…"), type "/" and pick it, or simply describe the task and Claude picks the matching tool.

| Tool | Use for | Try this prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision Coach** | Choices, tradeoffs & decisions | "Use Decision Coach. Help me choose between [option A] and [option B], given these priorities and constraints: [details]." |
| **Business Mentor** | Cross-functional business help | "Use Business Mentor. I have a problem that spans more than one area of my business. Help me diagnose it and identify practical next steps: [details]." |
| **GUIDE Mentoring** | Structured mentoring guidance | "Use GUIDE Mentoring. Help me work through this business challenge using a guided mentoring conversation: [challenge]." |
| **Mentee Self Guide** | Mentee prep & self-guidance | "Use Mentee Self Guide. Help me prepare for my next SCORE mentoring session. I want to make progress on [goal]." |

## Built-in rules

- The mentee owns the goals and choices. These tools ask, clarify, and recommend; they do not decide for the user.
- Each conversation is treated as being about one mentee. Details from other mentees are never brought in.
- Substantial work ends with a client-shareable **Mentor Session Summary** (Mentee Self Guide gives it when asked).
- Decision Coach gives a provisional direction with a confidence level, then pauses for the user's reaction on bigger decisions. Business Mentor knows every AI EHub tool and points to the right one.
