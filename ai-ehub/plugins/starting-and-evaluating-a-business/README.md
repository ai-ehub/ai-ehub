# Starting & Evaluating a Business — AI EHub

Four mentoring tools for founders, small-business owners, and the mentors who work with them. Each tool is a skill: ask for it by name in any chat (for example, "Use Startup Validator to…"), or simply describe the task and Claude picks the matching tool.

| Tool | Use for | Try this prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Startup Ideas** | Generate & refine ideas | "Use Startup Ideas. Generate and compare business concepts for [founder strengths/interests], serving [customer or geography]." |
| **Startup Validator** | Test an *idea* — a new startup concept or a growth idea for an existing business | "Use Startup Validator. I am considering [idea] for [customer] in [location]. Help me validate it before I invest further." |
| **Business Stress Test** | Test a *plan or a business* — a startup's execution plan, an existing business, or a major commitment | "Use Business Stress Test. Stress-test this plan or decision and identify the assumptions most likely to fail: [details]." |
| **Industry Insight** | Industry research & context, with current cited sources, one section at a time | "Use Industry Insight. Analyze the [industry] in [geography]. Give me a practical overview with current, cited evidence." |

## How the tools hand off

Startup Ideas → Startup Validator → Business Stress Test, with Industry Insight supplying research at any point. Each tool also knows the other AI EHub areas (Pricing & Finance, Marketing & Operations, Clarity & Decision) and will point to them by name when they fit better.

## Built-in rules

- Facts, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations are kept clearly separate; nothing is invented.
- Each conversation is treated as being about one business. Details from other mentees are never brought in.
- Substantial work ends with a client-shareable **Mentor Session Summary**.
- Industry Insight presents one section at a time and asks one decision-focused question after each. At the end it offers a **Complete Brief** containing only the directions agreed on in the session.
- Web search should be turned on for research that depends on current facts.
