# Pricing & Finance — AI EHub

Five mentoring tools for founders, small-business owners, and the mentors who work with them. Each tool is a skill: ask for it by name in any chat (for example, "Use Price Calculator to…"), type "/" and pick it, or simply describe the task and Claude picks the matching tool.

| Tool | Use for | Try this prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Price Calculator** | Pricing, margin & break-even | "Use Price Calculator. Calculate a selling price using these costs, fees, and target margin: [details]. Show the formula and assumptions." |
| **Financial Strategy** | Finance strategy & planning | "Use Financial Strategy. Review these business financials and help me understand cash flow, risks, and options: [figures]." |
| **Lender Navigator** | Explore financing options | "Use Lender Navigator. Help me identify financing paths and lender-fit questions for this need: [amount, use, location, business profile]." |
| **Deal Advisor** | Buy, sell & value businesses | "Use Deal Advisor. I am evaluating a business acquisition or sale. Here are the deal facts and questions: [details]." |
| **Grant Writing** | Grants, proposals & compliance | "Use Grant Writing. Help me assess this grant opportunity and prepare a compliant application: [grant details and business context]." |

## Built-in rules

- Facts, calculations, assumptions, and recommendations are kept clearly separate; nothing is invented, including lenders, grants, rates, or deadlines.
- Each conversation is treated as being about one business. Figures from other mentees are never brought in.
- Substantial work ends with a client-shareable **Mentor Session Summary** (Price Calculator offers it only for substantial work).
- Deal Advisor works in phases and stops to ask when key facts are missing. Grant Writing will not claim eligibility or compliance until the actual solicitation is in hand.
- Turn on web search for anything that depends on current programs, rates, fees, or deadlines.
